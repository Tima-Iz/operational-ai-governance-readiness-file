# Selected Research Outputs - Fatemeh Izadi

## Master's thesis (2026)
**Integrating Human Rights Due Diligence into Compliance for High-Risk AI Systems: A Critical Review of the EU AI Act through UNGP Principle 17**  
Roma Tre University. Degree awarded 7 July 2026. The thesis develops a traceable article-level mapping and two applied governance models connecting assessment, responsibility, evidence, communication, monitoring, and review.

## Accepted workshop contribution (2026)
**From Assessment to Governance: Why Article 27 FRIA Alone Cannot Secure Ongoing Fundamental Rights Protection**  
Accepted for presentation at the EVO Jean Monnet Chair research workshop, Roma Tre University, 14 September 2026.

## Open research portfolios

### [Operational AI Governance Readiness File](https://github.com/Tima-Iz/operational-ai-governance-readiness-file)
A publicly documented end-to-end governance file for a simulated high-risk employment AI deployment, including classification, inventory, risk register, FRIA-Plus, vendor assessment, evidence register, and decision record.

### [NGO Policy Briefing Agent](https://github.com/Tima-Iz/ngo-policy-briefing-agent)
A tested AI-assisted policy briefing workflow with source anchoring, evidence separation, stakeholder mapping, uncertainty notes, recommendations, evaluation rubrics, and human-review checkpoints.

## MBA thesis (2021)
**Identifying and Ranking Negotiation Tactics in the Shiraz City Council's Negotiation Discourse by AHP Method**  
Islamic Azad University. Mixed-method study using a four-round Delphi process and AHP; thesis grade 17.99/20.
