# Legal Status Register — EU AI Act Compliance File (Simulated Case)

> **SIMULATION NOTICE.** This project is a fictional compliance file created for
> portfolio and educational purposes. Not legal advice.

## Purpose

Before any artifact cites an instrument, this register answers: **what is its
legal status today?** The register prevents the most common failure in AI Act
writing — treating adopted-but-not-yet-applicable provisions, guidance, drafts,
standards, and the analyst's own framework as if they had the same force.

**Register date: 2026-07-13.** Statuses were checked on this date against the
sources in `methodology/source-register.md`; re-verify before each artifact is
finalized and before publication.

## Status categories used

| Category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **In force & applicable** | Binding and currently applies to the conduct in question |
| **In force, not yet applicable** | The instrument has entered into force but the specific provision's application date is in the future |
| **Adopted amendment, awaiting OJ publication** | Both co-legislators have adopted the text; it is not yet published in the Official Journal and not yet in force |
| **Published in OJ, not yet in force** | Published; the entry-into-force period is still running |
| **Binding law** | The instrument as such creates enforceable obligations (once/where applicable) |
| **Final non-binding guidance** | Adopted guidance; persuasive, not enforceable as law |
| **Draft guidance** | Not adopted; may change; context only |
| **Voluntary standard** | Compliance is optional; may support (not substitute) legal compliance |
| **Thesis-derived framework** | The analyst's own methodology (FRIA-Plus); no legal status whatsoever |

## Standing cautions

1. **The case sits in the deferral gap.** Modaretti deployed in January 2026.
   Annex III deployer obligations were never applicable before that date and,
   post-Omnibus, apply from 2 Dec 2027. GDPR and Art. 5 AI Act, by contrast,
   applied on day one. Artifacts must not describe Annex III obligations as
   currently binding on the parties — they are the *forthcoming* standard the
   compliance file prepares for, and good practice now.
2. **OJ publication is the outstanding procedural step** for the Omnibus.
   Before publishing this repository, re-check EUR-Lex (S-04) and update L-08,
   L-04, L-05, L-06 and the register date.
3. Rows marked **[unverified this project]** reflect the analyst's prior
   knowledge and must be verified at Tier 1 before an artifact conclusion
   rests on them (see `methodology/source-register.md`).
