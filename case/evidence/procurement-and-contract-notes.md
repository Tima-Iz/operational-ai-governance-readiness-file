Author: Procurement Manager (L. Ferraro) | Last updated: 20 January 2026

## 1. Procurement summary

- Need raised by HR Director (Oct 2025): application volume for store/logistics
  roles exceeds recruiter capacity; average time-to-shortlist 19 days.
- Options considered: TalentSift Recruit, one competing tool (RankWell),
  additional temporary recruiter headcount. TalentSift selected on price and
  integration speed. No formal weighted evaluation was documented (decision
  taken in vendor selection meeting, 18 Nov 2025 — see meeting note).
- Contract signed **15 December 2025**. Go-live **12 January 2026** following a
  compressed two-week onboarding (vendor's standard onboarding is 2–4 weeks;
  HR wanted go-live before the February seasonal intake).
- Tier: **Professional**, €48,000/year, 12-month initial term, auto-renewal.
- Data Processing Agreement (GDPR Art. 28) signed as annex 2 to the main
  agreement; TalentSift acts as processor, Modaretti as controller.

## 2. Contract extracts (Master Subscription Agreement, selected clauses)

**Clause 7.2 (Data retention).** "Candidate Data shall be retained for
thirty-six (36) months from last activity and thereafter deleted, unless the
Customer instructs otherwise in writing."

**Clause 9.3 (Compliance).** "Customer acknowledges that it remains solely
responsible for compliance with applicable employment, equal-treatment and
non-discrimination laws in its use of the Service and for all hiring decisions
made in connection with the Service."

**Clause 10.1 (Service changes).** "TalentSift may update, improve or modify
the Service, including its underlying models, at any time, provided the
overall functionality of the Service is not materially reduced."

**Clause 11.4 (Liability).** "Each party's aggregate liability under this
Agreement is capped at the fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the
claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss."

**Clause 13.2 (Audit).** "Customer may request one (1) remote audit per
contract year upon thirty (30) days' written notice. TalentSift may, at its
discretion, satisfy such request by providing a summary compliance report
prepared by TalentSift or its external auditor."
