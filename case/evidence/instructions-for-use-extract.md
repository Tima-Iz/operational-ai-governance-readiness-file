## Welcome to Recruit! (p. 1)

Congratulations on choosing TalentSift Recruit. This guide gets your team from
zero to a ranked pipeline in five steps. Most customers complete onboarding
within two weeks.

Recruit scores reflect objective candidate quality, so your recruiters can
trust the ranking and spend their energy where it matters: talking to great
candidates.

## Step 1 — Connect your careers site (p. 1)

Recruit integrates with all major ATS platforms and standard careers-site
forms. Your onboarding engineer will configure the connector. Supported input
formats: PDF, DOC/DOCX; application form fields are mapped during onboarding.

## Step 2 — Choose role templates (p. 2)

Select the closest template for each vacancy from the template library. You
can adjust the weighting sliders (Experience / Education / Skills /
Availability) per template. We recommend starting with the defaults, which
reflect what works across our customer base.

## Step 3 — Set your bands (p. 2)

Scores are grouped into bands: green (≥70), amber (40–69), red (<40) by
default. Thresholds are configurable per customer. Optional workflow
automations include auto-archiving of red-band candidates and scheduled
rejection e-mails — most high-volume customers enable both.

## Step 4 — Invite your team (p. 5)

Assign roles: Administrator, Recruiter, or Viewer. Viewers see shortlists
without scores — ideal for hiring managers.

## Step 5 — Go live (p. 5)

Your customer success manager will check in at 2 and 6 weeks. For best
results, we recommend that recruiters review borderline candidates
periodically.

## Good to know (p. 6)

- **Data retention:** candidate data is retained for 24 months by default,
  then deleted. Contact support to adjust.
- **Data location:** all data is processed and stored in the EU.
- **Uptime:** 99.9% SLA on all tiers. Status page: status.talentsift.example.
- **Updates:** scoring models are updated periodically to keep improving your
  results. Updates are applied automatically; no action is needed.
- **Support:** support@talentsift.example, weekdays 9:00–17:30 CET
  (Professional tier).
