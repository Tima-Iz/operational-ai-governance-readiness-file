**MODARETTI S.p.A. — DIREZIONE RISORSE UMANE**
**Procedura operativa: Screening candidature con TalentSift Recruit**
Document ref: HR-PROC-2026-04 | Version 1.2 | Date: 9 March 2026
Owner: HR Operations Lead (E. Guarneri) | Approved by: HR Director (S. Baldini)
Applies to: store roles and logistics roles, all Italian sites

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how the HR central recruitment team uses TalentSift
Recruit to screen and shortlist candidates. Screening is AI-assisted; all
hiring decisions are taken by people. Every application receives individual
consideration in line with our equal-opportunity commitments.

## 2. Process steps

1. Candidate applies via the careers portal (jobs.modaretti.example). The
   application transfers automatically to TalentSift Recruit.
2. Recruit assigns each candidate a match score (0–100) and a colour band:
   green (≥70), amber (40–69), red (<40).
3. The assigned recruiter reviews the vacancy pipeline at least twice per week.
4. The recruiter selects candidates from the green band (and amber where volume
   allows) for a 15-minute phone screen.
5. The recruiter forwards a shortlist of 5–8 candidates to the hiring manager
   (store manager or DC shift lead), who interviews and decides.
6. Offers are issued by HR Operations following the hiring manager's decision.

## 3. System configuration (agreed with TalentSift onboarding, Jan 2026)

- Role templates: "Sales Assistant — Fashion Retail", "Cashier", "Warehouse
  Operative", "Picker/Packer", "Floor Supervisor", "Shift Lead".
- Weighting adjustments: availability weighting increased to "high" for store
  roles (evening/weekend coverage is our main scheduling constraint).
- **Auto-archive:** red-band candidates (<40) are moved automatically to the
  "Not proceeding" folder. A standard rejection e-mail is sent automatically
  after 14 days unless the recruiter moves the candidate back into the
  pipeline before then.
- Recruiters may move any candidate between bands or restore candidates from
  "Not proceeding" at their discretion.

## 4. Roles and access

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| Central recruiters (4 FTE) | Full pipeline: scores, bands, rankings, highlight phrases |
| HR Operations Lead | Full pipeline + configuration settings |
| Hiring managers | Shortlisted candidates only (name, CV); no scores shown |
| Candidates | Application status only ("received" / "in progress" / "concluded") |

## 5. Candidate information

The careers portal privacy notice (updated December 2025) informs candidates
that applications are "processed using modern HR software to support our
recruitment team". Questions from candidates about screening are handled by
the recruiter using the standard FAQ sheet.

## 6. Training

All recruiters completed the TalentSift onboarding webinar (45 minutes,
recorded) in January 2026. New recruiters watch the recording during
induction.

## 7. Records and retention

Candidate data is retained and deleted in accordance with the Legal
department's retention schedule. System reports (time-to-shortlist,
pipeline volume) are exported monthly by HR Operations for management
reporting.

## 8. Review

This procedure is reviewed annually or upon significant system changes.
