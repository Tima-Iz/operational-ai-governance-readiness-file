# Hire smarter. TalentSift Recruit.

**TalentSift Recruit is the AI-powered screening platform that learns what
great hires look like — so your team can focus on people, not paperwork.**

## Why leading employers choose Recruit

- **92% screening accuracy.** Our models are trained on millions of real
  hiring outcomes across European retail, logistics, and services.
- **70% faster time-to-shortlist.** Recruiters using Recruit spend their time
  interviewing, not sifting.
- **Bias-free by design.** Protected attributes are removed before scoring.
  Every candidate is assessed on merit — nothing else.
- **A full audit trail of every decision**, built for enterprise accountability.
- **GDPR compliant and EU AI Act ready.** Compliance is engineered in, so you
  can deploy with confidence.

## How it works

1. **Connect** your careers site or ATS in under a day.
2. **Select** a role template from our library of 40+ proven profiles.
3. **Rank** — Recruit scores every applicant from 0 to 100 and surfaces the
   candidates most likely to succeed.
4. **Decide** — your team takes it from there. Recruit supports recruiters;
   it never replaces them.
